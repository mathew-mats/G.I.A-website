About Gadhafi Integrated Academy

A Muslim school community built on knowledge, faith, discipline and integrity — shaping the young leaders of tomorrow.

Faith-guided education. We nurture learners through Islamic values, academic excellence and character formation — preparing them for this life and the next.

Who We Are

An Institution Rooted in Faith, Excellence and Integrity

Gadhafi Integrated Academy is a Muslim secondary school in Kampala, Kawempe Mbogo, committed to providing quality education in a supportive, disciplined and faith-guided environment.

Our school nurtures learners academically, morally and spiritually — helping them discover their talents, develop practical skills, and prepare confidently for higher education and the world of work.

We believe education goes beyond the classroom. Through Islamic values, discipline, integrity, teamwork and dedication, we guide our learners to become responsible, God-fearing, self-reliant members of society.

What Guides Us

Mission, Vision and Core Values

Our Mission

To provide quality education that develops knowledgeable, disciplined and God-fearing learners prepared to contribute positively to society.

Our Vision

To nurture confident, responsible and successful learners who live by Islamic values and make a positive difference in their communities and beyond.

Our Values

Islamic values, integrity, discipline, respect, hard work, teamwork, responsibility and an unwavering commitment to excellence.
