Our Education

Preparing Learners for a Changing World

Gadhafi Integrated Academy offers a balanced academic program that combines rigorous classroom learning with Islamic values and practical, hands-on skills. Our approach follows Uganda's competence-based curriculum, placing the learner at the centre of every lesson.

We prepare students for national examinations at both O-Level and A-Level, while also equipping them with Quranic knowledge and vocational skills through our DIT training program. Every learner leaves our school with academic knowledge, moral grounding, practical ability and the discipline needed to succeed.
