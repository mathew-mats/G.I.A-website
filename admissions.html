Admissions at Gadhafi Integrated Academy

We welcome parents and guardians seeking a supportive, disciplined and faith-guided environment where learners can grow academically, morally and personally. Our admissions team is here to guide you every step of the way.

We admit students into Senior One (S1), Senior Five (S5) and, where space is available, into other classes through transfer.

What You Will Need

  • Completed PLE results slip (for S1) or UCE results slip (for S5)
  • Copy of the learner's birth certificate
  • Two passport-size photographs
  • Report card or transfer letter from previous school
  • Recommendation or Baptism/Islamic certificate (where applicable)
  • Filled and signed admission form

Education Options

Programs Available for Admission

From academic excellence to Quranic study and vocational skills — we have a program for every learner.

O-Level Education

Senior One to Senior Four, following the national competence-based curriculum and leading to UCE.

A-Level Education

Senior Five to Senior Six in arts, sciences and business streams — preparing learners for UACE and university.

Quran & Islamic Studies

Dedicated lessons in Quran recitation, Tajweed, Islamic studies and Arabic for learners at all levels.

DIT Skills Training

Certified practical and vocational training for learners interested in acquiring employable, hands-on skills.

Patriotism Club

An active patriotism club nurturing love for Uganda, service and responsible citizenship among learners.

Character Formation

Discipline, integrity and Islamic ethics at the heart of everything — shaping principled, God-fearing learners.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

When does admission open?
Admissions are open throughout the year, with the main intake at the beginning of the academic year. However, we recommend applying early as places fill up quickly.
Do you accept transfers from other schools?
Yes. Learners transferring from other schools are welcome. Please bring your transfer letter, report card and results slips for assessment.
Do you offer Quran and Islamic studies?
Yes. Quran recitation, Tajweed, Islamic studies and Arabic are core parts of school life at Gadhafi Integrated Academy. Learners at all levels receive dedicated Islamic instruction.
Do you accept learners of other faiths?
Yes. While we are a Muslim school, we welcome learners of all backgrounds. All students are expected to respect the school's Islamic values and discipline.
Do you offer boarding facilities?
Contact the school office for the latest information on boarding and day options, as availability may vary by term.
How much are the school fees?
Fees vary depending on the class and whether the learner is a day or boarding student. Please contact the school bursar for the current fee structure.
What subjects are offered at A-Level?
We offer combinations in sciences, arts and business, including Islamic studies and Arabic. Learners receive guidance during S4 to choose the best combination for their career goals.
Do you offer DIT skills training?
Yes. Our DIT program provides practical, certified vocational skills that prepare learners for employment or self-employment.
